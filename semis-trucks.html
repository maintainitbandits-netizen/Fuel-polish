Semi Truck & Commercial Fleet Fuel Polishing

Keep your fleet on the road with clean, high-quality diesel fuel. Our mobile service comes to your depot to polish fuel and clean tanks for semi trucks and commercial vehicles across Florida.

Professional Diesel Fuel Polishing for Semi Trucks & Fleets

For commercial trucking fleets, fuel quality directly impacts your bottom line. Contaminated diesel fuel causes clogged filters, injector damage, reduced fuel economy, and costly downtime. At Florida Fuel Polishing, we provide professional mobile fuel polishing for semi trucks, delivery fleets, and commercial vehicles across Florida.

Why Fleets Choose Florida Fuel Polishing

  • Reduce Downtime: Clean fuel means fewer clogged filters and fewer breakdowns.
  • Improve Fuel Economy: Removing water and contamination restores fuel quality and improves MPG.
  • Extend Engine Life: Clean fuel reduces wear on injectors, pumps, and engine components.
  • Save Money: Our four-step system saves 70-90% of existing fuel rather than requiring disposal.
  • Mobile On-Site Service: We come to your fleet depot or truck yard anywhere in Florida.

Our Fleet Fuel Polishing Process

Step 1: Pump out water, sludge, and contamination from diesel storage tanks and vehicle tanks.
Step 2: Filter fuel through two-stage filtration system down to 1 micron.
Step 3: Safe chemical cleaners dissolve varnish, gum, and deposits from tank walls.
Step 4: Rejuvenate fuel with algaecide, lubricant, water absorber, and stabilizer for 12-18 months.
