Marine Diesel Service

Complete marine diesel fuel system maintenance for yachts, fishing boats, and commercial vessels across Florida.

Keep Your Marine Diesel Engine Running Reliably

Your boat's diesel engine is only as good as the fuel going into it. Florida's warm, humid climate creates the perfect conditions for fuel contamination. At Florida Fuel Polishing, we specialize in marine diesel fuel system maintenance to keep your vessel running reliably.

Our Marine Diesel Services

  • Fuel Polishing — Remove water, algae, and sludge from your diesel fuel tank.
  • Fuel System Cleaning — Clean injectors, fuel lines, and filters.
  • Tank Cleaning — Remove buildup from tank walls and baffles.
  • Fuel Testing — Check for water, microbial growth, and fuel quality.
  • Biocide Treatment — Kill bacteria and algae in your fuel system.
  • Fuel Stabilization — Protect your fuel during off-season storage.

Why Marine Diesel Fuel Goes Bad

Diesel fuel in marine environments faces unique challenges: water condensation from humidity, microbial growth in warm temperatures, fuel degradation during long storage periods, and contamination from filling at various fuel docks. Regular maintenance is essential.
