Generator Fuel Polishing: NFPA 110 Compliance Guide

Published June 2026 | 6 min read

If you manage a facility with emergency backup generators, you've likely heard of NFPA 110. But do you know what it requires for your fuel system? This guide breaks down everything you need to know.

What Is NFPA 110?

NFPA 110 is the National Fire Protection Association's standard for emergency and standby power systems. It sets requirements for generator testing, maintenance, and fuel quality to ensure reliable operation during emergencies.

Fuel Requirements Under NFPA 110

Section 8.3 of NFPA 110 addresses fuel quality and maintenance. Key requirements include:

  • Fuel must be maintained in a clean, usable condition
  • Water and sediment must be removed from fuel tanks
  • Fuel must be tested regularly for quality
  • A fuel maintenance program must be documented

Annual Fuel Polishing

NFPA 110 strongly recommends annual fuel polishing for emergency generators. In Florida's humid climate, we recommend more frequent service — every 6-12 months — because condensation and microbial growth occur faster in warm, humid conditions.

Consequences of Non-Compliance

Failing to maintain fuel quality can result in:

  • Generator failure during power outages
  • Insurance claim denials
  • Violation of building codes
  • Legal liability if lives or property are at risk
