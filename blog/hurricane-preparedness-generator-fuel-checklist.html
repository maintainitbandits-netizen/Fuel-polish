Hurricane Preparedness: Generator Fuel Checklist

Published: June 1, 2026 | 4 min read

Is Your Generator Ready for Hurricane Season?

Hurricane season runs June 1 through November 30 in Florida. The time to check your generator fuel is now — not when a storm is bearing down on you. Here's our essential pre-hurricane checklist.

1. Test Your Fuel Quality

Don't assume your stored diesel is still good. Have it professionally tested or inspect it yourself. Look for water at the bottom of the tank, dark discoloration, sludge, or a foul smell (signs of microbial growth). If in doubt, call a professional for testing.

2. Schedule Fuel Polishing Before Storm Season

The best time to polish your generator fuel is early June, just before hurricane season peaks. Annual polishing removes accumulated water, sludge, and microbial contamination and stabilizes your fuel for the coming year.

3. Check Your Fuel Level

Keep your generator fuel tank at least 75% full during hurricane season. A full tank reduces condensation (less air space means less moisture) and ensures you have enough fuel for extended outages.

4. Inspect Fuel Filters and Separators

Replace fuel filters and service your water separator before hurricane season. If your fuel is contaminated, new filters will clog quickly. That's a sign you need fuel polishing.

5. Have a Backup Plan

Know who to call if your generator won't start during an emergency. Save our number: 904-767-1103. We provide emergency fuel polishing service throughout Florida.

Quick Hurricane Fuel Checklist

  • ✅ Fuel tested within last 30 days
  • ✅ Fuel polished within last 12 months
  • ✅ Tank at least 75% full
  • ✅ Fresh fuel filters installed
  • ✅ Water separator drained
  • ✅ Generator test-run for 30 minutes
  • ✅ Emergency contact saved in phone
