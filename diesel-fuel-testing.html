Diesel Fuel Testing Services

Professional fuel quality analysis and contamination testing for generators, boats, fleets, and storage tanks across Florida.

Know Exactly What's in Your Fuel

You can't fix a fuel problem you can't see. At Florida Fuel Polishing, we provide comprehensive diesel fuel testing to identify contamination before it damages your equipment. Our on-site testing gives you immediate answers.

Why Test Your Diesel Fuel?

  • Detect Water Contamination — Water is the #1 cause of microbial growth and fuel degradation.
  • Identify Microbial Growth — Bacteria, fungi, and algae can destroy your fuel system.
  • Check Fuel Stability — Old fuel can oxidize and form varnish that clogs injectors.
  • NFPA 110 Compliance — Regular fuel testing is required for emergency generator systems.
  • EPA Compliance — Ensure your fuel meets environmental standards.

What We Test For

  • Water Content: Free water, emulsified water, and dissolved water
  • Microbial Contamination: Bacteria, fungi, and yeast levels
  • Fuel Stability: Oxidation resistance and sediment formation
  • Particulate Contamination: Dirt, rust, and debris
  • Fuel Composition: Cetane number, sulfur content, and more
