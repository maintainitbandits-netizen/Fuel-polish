Hurricane Generator Fuel Checklist

Don't let contaminated fuel leave you powerless during a Florida hurricane. Follow this checklist to ensure your generator is ready.

Is Your Generator Ready for Hurricane Season?

Hurricane season in Florida runs from June 1 to November 30. The worst time to discover your generator has contaminated fuel is when a Category 3 storm is barreling toward your coast. At Florida Fuel Polishing, we help homeowners and businesses prepare their backup generators before disaster strikes.

Why Generator Fuel Goes Bad in Florida

Florida's heat and humidity accelerate fuel degradation. Diesel fuel can develop microbial growth within 3-6 months. Gasoline with ethanol absorbs moisture from the air. Without proper maintenance, your generator may not start when you need it most.

Pre-Hurricane Generator Fuel Checklist

☐ 1. Test Your Fuel Quality

Check for water at the bottom of your tank, algae growth, or fuel that smells sour or looks cloudy. If you see any signs of contamination, schedule fuel polishing immediately.

☐ 2. Schedule Fuel Polishing Before Storm Season

NFPA 110 recommends annual fuel polishing. But in Florida's climate, we recommend every 6-12 months. Schedule your polishing in April or May — before hurricane season begins.

☐ 3. Check Your Fuel Level

Keep your generator fuel tank at least 75% full during hurricane season. An empty tank has more air space, which means more condensation and water buildup. However, don't top it off — leave room for fuel expansion.

☐ 4. Inspect Fuel Filters and Separators

Replace fuel filters before hurricane season. Clogged filters are the #1 reason generators fail to start during power outages.

☐ 5. Add Fuel Stabilizer

Use a high-quality fuel stabilizer to keep your fuel fresh during storage. Our proprietary additive package includes stabilizer that protects fuel for 12-18 months.

☐ 6. Run a Full System Test

Run your generator under load for at least 30 minutes before hurricane season to verify everything works. This also helps circulate fresh fuel through your system.
