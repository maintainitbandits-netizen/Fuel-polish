Boat & Marine Fuel Polishing

Don't let bad fuel leave you stranded on the water. We come to your dock or marina to clean your boat's fuel system.

Keep Your Boat's Engine Running Smoothly

Florida's warm, humid climate is a breeding ground for fuel contamination. Water condenses in your boat's fuel tank, creating the perfect environment for bacteria, fungi, and algae. The result? Clogged filters, damaged injectors, and engine failure when you're miles offshore.

At Florida Fuel Polishing, we come directly to your dock, slip, or storage location anywhere in Florida. Our mobile service cleans your fuel tank and restores your fuel — saving you thousands in repairs and disposal costs.

The Problem with Boat Fuel in Florida

  • Microbial Growth: Florida's heat accelerates bacteria and algae growth in diesel and gas tanks.
  • Water Contamination: Humidity causes condensation inside fuel tanks.
  • Ethanol Issues: Gasoline with ethanol attracts water and causes phase separation.
  • Fuel Degradation: Stored diesel fuel oxidizes, forming varnish and gum.
