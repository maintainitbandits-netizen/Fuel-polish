Generator Fuel Polishing

Keep your emergency backup generators ready. We clean diesel fuel tanks so your generators start when you need them most.

Don't Let Bad Fuel Leave You in the Dark

Contaminated fuel is the #1 cause of emergency generator failure. When the power goes out during a Florida hurricane or grid failure, your generator needs to start immediately. But water, algae, and sludge in your diesel fuel tank can turn your generator into an expensive paperweight.

At Florida Fuel Polishing, we specialize in restoring diesel fuel in emergency backup generators. Our mobile service comes to your facility anywhere in Florida to clean your tanks and restore your fuel.

Why Florida Generators Need Fuel Polishing

Florida's hot, humid climate creates the perfect conditions for microbial growth in diesel fuel. Bacteria and algae thrive in the water that condenses in fuel tanks. Without regular polishing, your generator's fuel becomes contaminated and unusable.

  • NFPA 110 Compliance: Annual fuel polishing is required for emergency power systems.
  • Hurricane Preparedness: Don't discover bad fuel during a storm. Polish before hurricane season.
  • Prevents Costly Repairs: Clean fuel protects injectors, pumps, and engines from damage.
  • Saves 70-90% of Fuel: We restore existing fuel instead of requiring disposal and replacement.
