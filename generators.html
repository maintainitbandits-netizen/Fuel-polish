Professional Generator Fuel Polishing in Florida

When the power goes out, your emergency generator is your lifeline. But contaminated fuel is the #1 cause of generator failure during emergencies. At Florida Fuel Polishing, we specialize in cleaning and restoring diesel fuel in emergency backup generator systems across the state of Florida.

Florida's hot, humid climate is the perfect environment for microbial growth in diesel fuel. Bacteria, fungi, and algae thrive in the water that condenses in fuel tanks, creating sludge that clogs filters, injectors, and fuel lines. When you need your generator most during a hurricane, grid failure, or emergency contaminated fuel can leave you in the dark.

Why Generator Fuel Polishing is Critical in Florida

Florida's tropical climate creates unique challenges for generator fuel systems. High humidity causes condensation in fuel tanks, introducing water that promotes microbial growth. Temperatures year-round above 70°F accelerate bacteria and algae proliferation. Without regular fuel polishing, your emergency generator's fuel can become unusable within months.

  • NFPA 110 Compliance: Our fuel polishing services help you meet NFPA 110 standards for emergency power system maintenance.
  • Prevents Costly Downtime: Clean fuel means reliable generator startup when you need it most.
  • Extends Equipment Life: Removing water, sludge, and microbial contamination reduces wear on fuel injectors, pumps, and engines.
  • Environmental Responsibility: We save 70-90% of your existing fuel rather than pumping it out for disposal.

Our Generator Fuel Polishing Process

Step 1: We pump out water, sludge, and crud from the bottom of your generator fuel tank.
Step 2: We filter your remaining fuel through a state-of-the-art two-stage filtration system down to 1 micron.
Step 3: While filtering, we inject proven safe chemical cleaners that dissolve varnish, sludge, and buildup from tank walls and behind baffles.
Step 4: We rejuvenate your fuel with our proprietary additive package including algaecide, lubricant, water absorber, and stabilizer that protects your fuel for 12-18 months.
