How It Works

Our 4-Step Fuel Polishing Process

Most companies use a basic 2-step filter. Our advanced 4-step system saves 70-90% of your old fuel and keeps it fresh for 12-18 months.

1

Pump Out Contaminants

We remove water, sludge, and debris from the bottom of your tank for proper disposal.

2

Two-Stage Filtration

Your fuel is filtered through our advanced system down to 1 micron.

3

Chemical Tank Cleaning

We inject cleaners that dissolve varnish and sludge from tank walls. Other companies skip this step.

4

Fuel Rejuvenation

Your fuel gets our proprietary additive package — stabilized and protected for 12-18 months.
