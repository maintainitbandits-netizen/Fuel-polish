How We Work

Our Advanced Four-Step Fuel Polishing Process

Unlike other companies that use a basic two-step system, our proven four-step method saves 70-90% of your old fuel and ensures your tank is truly clean.

1

Pump Out Contaminants

We first pump out all water, sludge, and crud from the bottom of your tank for proper disposal.

2

Two-Stage Filtration

We filter your remaining fuel through a sophisticated two-stage filtration system down to 1 micron.

3

Chemical Cleaning

We inject proven safe chemical cleaners to dissolve varnish, sludge, and crud from tank walls.

4

Fuel Rejuvenation

We rejuvenate your fuel with algaecide, booster, lubricant, water absorber, and stabilizer for 12-18 months protection.
